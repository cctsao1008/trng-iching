## Textual evidence and limits

- [Shangshu, Hongfan](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/尚書/洪範), first category: 水曰潤下、火曰炎上、木曰曲直、金曰從革、土爰稼穡. Functional/material analogies; not physical equations.
- [Huainanzi, Tianwen Xun](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/淮南子/天文訓), 卷三, passage 何謂五星: explicit associations wood/Jupiter/east/spring, fire/Mars/south/summer, earth/Saturn/center, metal/Venus/west/autumn, water/Mercury/north/winter. A more direct classical basis for a planetary track than assigning modern instruments to phases. This is historical cosmology, not validated causal influence.
- [Yisi Zhan, volume 10, sections 68–69](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=乙巳占/10&oldid=1538300): actual text reviewed. Wind observation takes an exposed site, unobstructed mounting, instrument weight and sensitivity, direction, onset/end, duration, strength and local wind-prone conditions into account. The text distinguishes normal local winds from unusual events and excludes some concurrent weather conditions from a rule. Modern methodological analogies: site/support, instrument response, event definition, context-conditioned reference and abstention. Ancient quantitative range and omen claims are not validated here; do not adopt them as calibration or a prediction model.
- [Huangji Jingshi, volume 11, Observing Things 52–54](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/皇極經世/卷十一): observer senses, objects and functions, four seasonal stages 生長收藏, combinatorial representation of stages. [Volume 13](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/皇極經世書/卷十三) distinguishes 384 line count from 360 operational count. [Huangji Jingshi Guanwu Waipian Yanyi, volume 1](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/皇極經世觀物外篇衍義_(四庫全書本)/卷1) is a later commentary, not Shao Yong's original prose; discusses 12 hui per yuan and 360 yun per yuan and nested cycles. Conventional 12*30*12*30=129600 year scheme is symbolic time arithmetic, not astronomical period evidence. Online received texts are incomplete in places; no full edition comparison completed.
- [Xici II chapters 1–4,7–8](https://zh.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=易傳/繫辭下&oldid=2611136) and [Shuogua chapters 1–7](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/周易/說卦): images, motion, seasonal alternation, time/position and doubled three powers. Neither prescribes a numeric threshold, five physical channels or quantum top line.

## T01/T02/T06 semantic algebra

R-Trend: encode sign(b-a), sign(c-b); primary earlier trend and relating later trend. J-S: encode sign(s_current), sign(s_current-s_previous), s=x-r; primary residual and relating trend. State transition: encode sign(s_previous), sign(s_current); moving iff opposite nonzero state signs. All use pair mapping (--)=8,(-+)=6,(+-)=9,(++)=7, zero/unresolved abstains. Relating is not a forecast in any of these definitions.

Actual exhaustive integer diagnostic, a,b,c,r in {-2,-1,0,1,2}, constant reference r, 625 tuples, deterministic JavaScript evaluation (no observations). All three resolved on 240, all same on only 40. R/J jointly resolved320 differ180; R/transition240 differ130; J/transition300 differ200. Common positive affine x->3x+7 (reference transformed) and common strictly increasing nonlinear x->x^3 fixed reference: zero invariance failures for all three. State transition moving iff reference crossing: zero failures. Time-varying reference breaks J-S nonlinear invariance: prev x=2,r=0, current x=4,r=3 gives residual 2->1 and line9; square both positive-domain quantities gives 4->7 and line7. Common positive affine still preserves residual signs and differences if reference transformed consistently. This is algebraic semantics, not empirical performance or a probability sample. Explicitly record unit/scale/orientation and uncertainty; positive gain changes units, inversion changes convention. J-S moving is opposition, not a proven imminent crossing; transition moving is an observed crossing, not proof of future behavior.
